Logo usage rules
✗

No all-caps wordmark

Always "Local Legend" title case. Never LOCAL LEGEND. The wordmark is set at the component level — do not override its text transform.

✗

Shield stays Legend Blue

Shield = blue, or ink on blue surfaces. Lime lives on the star and pin only. Never put lime on the shield in standard use.

✗

No stretching or harsh shadows

Soft and rounded, never distorted. No drop shadows applied inside the mark itself. No recoloring the star or LL.

→

Clear space = X

Keep a margin equal to the badge star height on all four sides. Nothing intrudes into this zone.

→

Minimum sizes

Horizontal lockup: 160px wide minimum. Icon badge: 32px minimum. Favicon use: 16px. Below these sizes, legibility breaks.

→

Video bug placement

Use a 42×42px rounded-square tile (border-radius: 12px) in Legend Blue, with ink-lime badge inside. Top-left corner of video frames.

06

Voice & Copy Rules

The cool friend who is great at this and makes it look easy. Confident, never corporate. Empowers the client — the hero is always them, not the studio.

✓ DO

Short, punchy sentences. Plain English. The message is always "look how easy this is," never "look how clever we are."

✗ DON'T

No em dashes. Use commas, periods, parentheses, or hyphens. Em dashes are a hard block everywhere in the brand.

✓ DO

Roast the old way, kindly. Poke fun at shaky selfie clips and posting once a season. Show the better way.

✗ DON'T

No jargon, hype, or fake urgency. No "game-changing," "revolutionary," or countdown timers. Human and honest.

✓ DO

Demystify AI and video. Make it feel approachable and exciting. The client films nothing and just posts.

✗ DON'T

No talking down. The audience are smart owner-operators. They decide fast and value credibility. Never condescending.

Sample lines — on-brand copy

Sections (in order)

  • Nav — sticky, ink/cream bg with blur, horizontal lockup left, lime CTA pill right

Visual treatment

  • Dark-first — default background is Ink Navy (#0E1016)
  • Alternate bands — use Ink 700 (#1D212B) for section variety
  • Cream bands — one or two sections can be full cream bg for contrast
  • Lime only as accent — never a full background, always a pop detail
  • Border radii — cards use r-lg (32px), buttons always r-pill (999px)
  • Shadows — blue buttons get shadow-blue, lime gets shadow-lime
  • Max-width — 1240px, padding 32px on each side
  • Mobile-first — hero stacks to single column below 768px

08

Guardrails

Non-negotiables that apply to every surface, every screen, every line of copy.

✗

No em dashes — ever

Use commas, periods, parentheses, or hyphens. This is a hard rule in copy, UI labels, headlines, everything.

✗

No lime backgrounds

Signal Lime is an accent and pop color. Never use it as a page background, section fill, or card background.

✗

No harsh edges or shadows

Soft and rounded everywhere. No sharp 0px corners on UI elements. No heavy box shadows with dark spread.

✗

No tiny type

18px minimum body. 14px minimum caption/label. Assume low vision throughout. High contrast mandatory.

✗

No all-caps wordmark

Always "Local Legend" in title case. The wordmark font is set at the component level and must not be overridden.

✗

No invented brand colors

Stick to the five core colors and their defined tints. New hues break the system. Tints must be derived, not invented.

→

Domain & handle

Domain: locallegend.studio. Handle: @local_legend_yvr. First market: Vancouver. Never reference other cities unless specified.

→

One agent per area

→

Self-contained brand

Do not reference, link to, or borrow visual language from any other brand or service. Local Legend stands entirely alone.
